HOW IT WORKS

Structured coordination instead of scattered posts

The workflow is designed around discoverability, privacy, lifecycle tracking, and exact-group matching.

Create a structured request with blood group, units, hospital/area, urgency, and needed time.

PRIVACY & SAFETY

Designed for coordination—not clinical decisions.

The platform deliberately separates community matching from medical eligibility. A blood-group label on a profile is not proof that someone is medically eligible to donate.
